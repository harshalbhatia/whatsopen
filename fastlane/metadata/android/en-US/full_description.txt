WhatsOpen lets you start a WhatsApp conversation with any phone number — even numbers you haven't saved as contacts.

Features:

* Type a number and open the chat; your country code is filled in
* Paste text with phone numbers in it and WhatsOpen finds each one
* Open a chat with a recent caller, with filters for incoming, missed and non-contact calls
* Share text, tap a phone link, or select a number in any app to open its chat
* Paste & Open from a home screen shortcut or a Quick Settings tile
* Works with WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business
* Material You colours, dark theme and a themed icon

WhatsOpen requires no network access, contains no analytics, and does not track you. All processing happens on-device.

Permissions:

* Read call log (optional) — to show recent calls. Names come from the call log itself; WhatsOpen doesn't read your contacts.
